## 2. Booking Rules, Fees and Policies

### 2.1 Guest numbers and occupancy

Every apartment has a **base occupancy** and a **maximum occupancy** (see the table in section 1.2). You may never exceed the maximum, even for a few hours. Infants under 2 years old sleep free and do not count as guests. Children aged 2 and above count as guests.

Only guests registered on the booking may stay overnight. If your plans change and more people will sleep in the apartment, tell the host before they arrive so the booking can be updated (subject to the maximum occupancy and to section 2.2).

### 2.2 Extra guest fee

If the number of guests is above the base occupancy of the apartment, an extra guest fee of **PKR 2,000 per extra guest per night** applies, up to the maximum occupancy. For example, three guests in the One-Bedroom (base 2) pay one extra guest fee each night, and five guests in the Two-Bedroom (base 4) pay one extra guest fee each night. The Studio has no extra guest fee because its base and maximum are both 2.

### 2.3 What is included in the nightly rate, and optional fees

Your nightly rate already includes electricity, piped gas, water, Wi-Fi, the final cleaning fee, bed linen, towels and all taxes. **There is no separate cleaning fee.** Nightly rates change with the season and are shown when you book.

### 2.4 Security deposit (direct bookings only)

For **direct bookings** (booked with us and not through a platform) we collect a refundable security deposit of **PKR 25,000**. Pay it by bank transfer or mobile wallet (Easypaisa or JazzCash) at least 24 hours before arrival. The host sends the payment details. After you check out and we inspect the apartment, the deposit is refunded to the same account within **3 business days**, minus the cost of any damage or fees described in section 11.7.

For bookings made through **Airbnb or Booking.com** we do not collect a deposit. The platform's own damage-protection rules apply instead.

### 2.5 Paying for your booking

- **Bookings made through a platform** (Airbnb, Booking.com): you pay through the platform, and the platform's payment rules apply.
- **Direct bookings:** 50% of the total is due to confirm the booking, and the balance is due **48 hours before arrival**. Payment is by bank transfer or mobile wallet. Our front desk does not handle cash.

### 2.6 Cancellation policy

- **Direct bookings:** free cancellation with a full refund up to **7 days before the arrival date**. If you cancel between 7 days and 48 hours before arrival, you receive a 50% refund. Within 48 hours of arrival there is no refund. Refunds are processed within 5 business days to the original payment method.
- **Bookings made through Airbnb or Booking.com:** the cancellation policy shown on your platform booking applies, not the direct-booking policy above. Cancel and request refunds through the platform.

Refund exceptions (illness, flight problems and similar) are decided by the host. See section 2.8.

### 2.7 Changing or extending your stay

- **Extending:** ask us. If the apartment is free for the extra nights we can usually extend at the same nightly rate. The host confirms every extension personally; it is not confirmed until the host says so.
- **Changing dates or moving to another apartment:** subject to availability and the host's approval.
- **Minimum and maximum stay:** the minimum is 1 night and the maximum is **28 nights** per booking. Longer stays are only possible with the host's approval.
- **Special rates and long-stay discounts:** we do not publish any. The host decides case by case.

### 2.8 Decisions only the host can make

The front desk, the guards and automated assistants cannot promise any of the following. Only the host decides, so requests are passed to the host:

- refunds, compensation or goodwill gestures
- special rates or long-stay discounts
- exceptions to check-in or check-out times
- changes to booking dates or moving to a different apartment
- deductions from the security deposit
- stays longer than 28 nights

### 3.4 Guest registration and ID

Pakistani law and our building rules require us to register every adult guest. Before arrival, every guest aged 18 or over must send:

- **Pakistani nationals:** a clear photo of the CNIC (front and back).
- **Foreign nationals:** a photo of the passport photo page and the visa page.

The host sends an upload link when you book. Please complete it **at least 24 hours before arrival**. The primary guest on the booking must be 21 or older. **Your door code is only released after your ID has been verified** (section 3.5).

Check-in time is **2:00 PM**. The apartments use a smart keypad lock, so no one has to meet you.

1. Arrive at Gate 2 and tell the guard your apartment number. The guard will register you.
2. Take the lift to your floor.
3. On the day of arrival, once your ID is verified (and, for direct bookings, your balance is paid), the host messages you a personal 6-digit door code.
4. Touch the keypad to wake it, enter the code, then press the **#** key.

Your code works from 2:00 PM on the arrival day until 11:00 AM on the departure day. The front desk can help in person between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM. Door codes are only ever sent by the host to the person on the booking. Nobody else, including the front desk and guards, will tell a code to anyone who asks.

### 3.6 Early check-in and luggage storage

- **Early check-in** is possible **from 12:00 PM** for **PKR 2,000**, but only if the apartment was empty the night before. Request it **at least 24 hours before arrival**. The host confirms by 10:00 AM on the day of arrival. Check-in before 12:00 PM is not possible because the apartment is being cleaned.
- **Luggage storage** is free at the front desk before check-in and after check-out, between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM. Please do not leave valuables, passports or cash. Outside front desk hours, ask the security desk; luggage is left at your own risk.

### 3.7 Late arrival

Self check-in works at any hour. If you will arrive after **11:00 PM**, tell the host in advance so the guard expects you. The guards at Gate 2 are on duty all night, but the front desk is closed after 9:00 PM.

On arrival you will find two 1.5-litre bottles of drinking water, tea bags, instant coffee, sugar and salt, soap and shampoo, toilet paper, dish soap, a bottle of laundry detergent (units with a washing machine), bin bags, and fresh towels (one bath towel per guest plus one extra). ## 6. Electricity, Load-Shedding and the Generator

### 6.1 Power cuts (load-shedding)

Like most of Lahore, the grid power can be cut for short periods. The building has an **automatic backup generator that starts within about 30 seconds** of a power cut. You will notice a brief interruption and then the power returns.

### 6.2 What works on the generator

Running on the generator: lights, sockets, fridge, TV, Wi-Fi router, lifts, water pump, the gas hob, and **one air conditioner per apartment** (marked with a green sticker; the Penthouse has two).

**Not running on the generator** (because of power limits): the induction plate, microwave, oven, electric kettle, air fryer, hair dryer, washing machine and the water heater (geyser). During a cut, cook on the gas hob. Hot water will not heat during a power cut, but water already hot stays hot for a few hours.

### 6.3 If the power goes off only in your apartment

Check the breaker board in the closet near your entrance door. Flip any switch that is down back up. If it trips again, unplug heavy appliances and call the emergency maintenance line. Running the kettle, microwave and induction plate at the same time can overload the kitchen circuit.

## 7. Cooling, Heating, Hot Water and Drinking Water

### 7.1 Air conditioning

All ACs are inverter split units. Use the remote, set the mode to **Cool** and a temperature of **24 to 26 °C** for the best comfort and lowest noise. Keep windows and balcony doors closed while the AC runs. The remote batteries (AAA) are in the kitchen drawer. Please switch the AC off when you leave the apartment.

### 7.2 Winter heating

From December to February the ACs can heat. Set the remote mode to **Heat**. We also provide **free electric oil-filled heaters** on request from the front desk (one per bedroom). We do not provide gas heaters, for safety reasons.

### 7.3 Hot water (geyser)

Hot water comes from an electric geyser (storage water heater). The switch is on the wall outside the bathroom door. **Switch it on about 30 minutes before you need hot water.** A timer automatically cuts the power after 2 hours, so switch it on again if you need more. Geyser sizes: Studio 35 litres, One-Bedroom 50 litres, Two-Bedroom 80 litres, Penthouse two 80-litre units. After heavy use, allow about 30 minutes for reheating. We use electric geysers because piped gas pressure in Lahore can be low, especially on winter mornings.

### 7.4 Water supply

Water comes from a rooftop tank and an automatic booster pump, available 24 hours. Occasionally the building cleans the tank; we announce it at least a day ahead.

### 7.5 Drinking water

**Do not drink the tap water.** Every kitchen has a filtered (reverse osmosis, RO) water dispenser with hot and cold water, and two bottled waters are waiting on arrival. Please use the filtered water for drinking, tea and cooking rice or pasta.

## 8. Kitchen and Cooking

### 8.1 Cooking equipment

Each kitchen has a gas hob, an induction plate, a microwave, a kettle and a toaster. The One-Bedroom, Two-Bedroom and Penthouse also have an air fryer. Only the Two-Bedroom and the Penthouse have an oven. We provide pots, pans, a knife set, chopping board, plates, glasses, cups and cutlery for the maximum number of guests.

### 8.2 Using the gas hob

Piped gas supplies the hob. In winter the gas pressure can be low early in the morning and the flame may look weak. This is normal; use the induction plate instead. To light the hob, press and turn the knob, and hold it for a few seconds. **Always turn the hob off when you leave the kitchen.** The red gas shut-off lever is under the kitchen sink (section 12.3).

### 8.3 Supplies

We supply tea, instant coffee, sugar, salt, pepper, cooking oil, dish soap and a sponge. We do not provide fresh milk or groceries. Section 17 lists the nearest shop.

### 8.4 Fridge, cooking smells and the dishes

Please wash your dishes before check-out. There is no dishwasher. Use the exhaust fan when frying. Please do not pour oil down the sink; let it cool and put it in the bin.

### 8.5 Cooking Pakistani dishes without setting off the alarm

Frying onions, tempering spices (tadka) and making parathas can create smoke that triggers the smoke detector. Before you start, switch on the exhaust fan and open a window, and use the lowest flame that works. **Never cover or disable the smoke detector.** If the alarm sounds from cooking smoke, fan the detector with a towel and open the balcony door; it stops by itself within a minute. If you see actual flames, use the kitchen fire extinguisher or a damp cloth over the pan, leave the apartment and follow section 12.2.

## 11. House Rules

Quiet hours are **10:00 PM to 8:00 AM**. Please keep music, TV and voices low, and avoid noise in the corridors and on the balconies. Residents include families with children and elderly people.

Smoking and vaping are **not allowed** inside the apartments, on the balconies, on the Penthouse terrace, in the corridors, in the lifts or in the basement. You may smoke only in the **marked corner at the north end of the roof terrace**. ### 11.3 Alcohol and prohibited items

Alcohol, drugs, weapons and any illegal items are not allowed anywhere in the building. Guests must follow Pakistani law and the building by-laws at all times.

### 11.4 Visitors

Day visitors are welcome **between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM**. Every visitor must register at the Gate 2 guard desk with a CNIC or passport, and you must collect them from the gate. Pets are **not allowed** in the apartments or in the building. Certified service animals are welcome with the host's prior approval; please tell the host before you book.

### 11.6 Parties and events

Parties, events, celebrations and gatherings larger than your registered guests plus the visitor limit are **not allowed**. A violation can lead to an immediate end of your stay without a refund and loss of the security deposit.

Please report breakages straight away. Accidental breakage of small items (a glass or plate) is not a problem if you tell us. ### 11.8 Open flames and barbecues

Barbecues, charcoal grills, candles with open flames and fireworks are **not allowed** anywhere in the building, including balconies, the roof terrace and the Penthouse terrace. This is a fire-safety rule.

### 11.9 Respecting neighbours and local customs

Please dress modestly in the lobby, corridors and lifts, avoid blocking the corridors, and greet the guards and staff; they are here to help you. Do not photograph other residents or the staff without their permission.

Call **1122** for emergencies. The nearest hospital with a 24-hour emergency department is Gulberg Community Hospital, about 2.5 km away (roughly 10 minutes by car). A 24-hour pharmacy is a 3-minute walk away (section 17).

A first-aid kit is on the top shelf of the entrance closet in every apartment. It contains plasters, bandages, antiseptic wipes, paracetamol and rehydration salts.

### 12.6 Security

Guards are on duty at both gates 24 hours a day. Lock the door whenever you leave the apartment and keep windows and balcony doors locked at night. The building does not accept responsibility for valuables left in the apartment or in the basement.

## 13. Maintenance and Troubleshooting

### 13.1 How to report a problem

For anything non-urgent, message the host on WhatsApp with a photo if you can. For urgent problems, call the emergency maintenance line (section 1.3).

### 13.2 How fast we respond

- **Urgent** (no power in your apartment, no water, a gas smell, flooding, a lock-out): within **60 minutes** between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM, and as fast as possible overnight.
- **Standard** (a light bulb, a dripping tap, a noisy AC): the same day if reported before 4:00 PM, otherwise the next morning.

## 16. Seasonal Notes

### 16.1 Smog season (roughly November to January)

Lahore can have heavy smog in winter. On bad days, keep windows and balcony doors closed and run the air purifier (switched on with its power button; the filter is changed monthly). Plan outdoor activities for the afternoon, when the air is usually clearer.

### 16.2 Summer heat

From May to September the temperature is very high. Keep curtains closed in the afternoon, use the AC at 24 to 26 °C, and drink plenty of filtered water.

### 16.3 Ramadan

During Ramadan the front desk opens from **10:00 AM to 6:00 PM**. We ask guests not to eat, drink or smoke in the corridors, lobby and on the roof terrace during fasting hours, out of respect for residents. Quiet hours are unchanged.

### 16.4 Eid holidays

On the first 2 days of Eid ul Fitr and the first 3 days of Eid ul Adha the front desk is closed. The host, the emergency maintenance line and the guards are available as usual. Book your arrival and shopping early because many shops close.

## 19. Appliance How-To Guide

### 19.1 The smart door lock

The keypad is on the apartment door at handle height. Touch it with your palm to wake it, then type your 6-digit code and press **#**. A green light and a short beep mean the door is unlocked; a red light and a long beep mean the code was wrong. After three wrong tries the keypad locks itself for 5 minutes. From the inside, turn the thumb-turn knob to lock or unlock. The door locks automatically about 10 seconds after it closes. The lock runs on 4 AA batteries; if the keypad beeps three times when you wake it, the batteries are low and you should tell the host. A low battery will not lock you out immediately.

### 19.2 The air-conditioner remote

Press **ON/OFF**, then **MODE** to choose Cool (snowflake), Heat (sun), Fan or Dry (droplet). Use the up and down arrows to set the temperature. The **SLEEP** button slowly raises the temperature at night so you are not too cold. If the AC beeps but the room does not cool, check that the remote shows Cool and not Fan. Ice on the indoor unit usually means the temperature is set too low or a window is open; switch to Fan for 20 minutes to let it melt.

### 19.3 Induction plate and microwave

The induction plate only works with flat-bottomed magnetic pans (the provided pots and pans work). It does not work with aluminium or glass pans. Use metal-free dishes in the microwave and never run it empty. Remember that the induction plate, microwave and kettle do not run on the generator during a power cut (section 6.2), and that running them all at once can trip the kitchen breaker (section 6.3).

### 19.4 Washing machine and washer-dryer

The washing machines in the One-Bedroom and Two-Bedroom take up to 7 kg. Use the **Quick 30** programme for lightly worn clothes and the **Cotton 40 °C** programme for normal loads. One cap of the provided detergent is enough. The Penthouse washer-dryer washes up to 8 kg and dries up to 4 kg; a wash-and-dry cycle takes about 4 hours. Washing machines do not run on the generator, and a cycle interrupted by a power cut resumes automatically when the grid power returns. Please clean the lint filter of the washer-dryer after each dry cycle.

### 19.5 The water dispenser

The filtered water dispenser in the kitchen has two taps: blue for cold and red for hot. The hot tap has a child-lock: press and hold the safety button while you press the tap. The dispenser needs mains power, so after a power cut it takes a few minutes to restart. If the dispenser leaks or the water tastes unusual, stop using it and message the host.

### 19.6 The air purifier

Switch the air purifier on with the power button and use the speed button to choose Low, Medium or High. The indicator ring shows air quality: **green** is good, **orange** is moderate and **red** is poor. Keep the doors and windows of the room closed while it runs. The purifier is quiet on Low and suitable for sleeping.

### 19.7 The intercom

The intercom panel is next to the entrance door. Press **Front Desk** to call the lobby (9:00 AM to 9:00 PM) or **Security** to call the guard desk (24 hours). When a visitor arrives at Gate 2, the guard calls your intercom to ask whether to let them in.
